Brainstorm to Action

Paste the sticky notes from any brainstorm or retro. The model sorts every note into action items, decisions, open questions, problems and ideas, then pulls out the owner and due date of each action and flags the ones nobody owns. It runs entirely in your browser, so your notes never leave the page.

How well it works

Tested on 124 hand-written sticky notes from 8 boards that the model never saw in training. Compared with a keyword-rules baseline.

89%of action items caught (keyword rules: 36%)
81%of notes flagged as actions really are actions
25 of 28action items with owner and due date both right
68%overall note-type accuracy (keyword rules: 54%)
<10 msto sort a 16-note board, no server, $0 per board

Weak spots: telling ideas apart from decisions (F1 0.44 and 0.45), since both are often written as short statements. Automatic theme grouping was tested and did not meet the quality bar, so it is left out of this version.
